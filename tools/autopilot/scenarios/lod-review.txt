# Structure writer LODs (T-031): lf_lod_post is one drawable with four LODs, 48, 24, 12 and 6 sides, each sampling its own
# texture quadrant: LOD 0 green with one dark band, LOD 1 yellow with two, LOD 2 orange with three, LOD 3 red with four.
# asset.json writes the LOD distances 12, 25, 50 and 100 m into drawable +0x50 (draw distance 150 m). The camera stops
# between them (6, 18, 38, 75 m), then beyond the last (130 m), so each shot shows which LOD the game draws there.
# Whether the game switches by these distances, by its own, or not at all is what this scenario finds out.
god on
wanted 0
goto east_park
wait 5000
time 13 0
weather 1
spawnprop lf_lod_post 4 0
expect "autopilot_prop handle=\d+ model=lf_lod_post" 15
wait 1500
hud off
cam prop 0 6 1.5
wait 2500
shot lod_6m
cam prop 0 18 2.5
wait 2500
shot lod_18m
cam prop 0 38 4
wait 2500
shot lod_38m
cam prop 0 75 6
wait 2500
shot lod_75m
cam prop 0 130 9
wait 2500
shot lod_130m
cam off
hud on
clear
